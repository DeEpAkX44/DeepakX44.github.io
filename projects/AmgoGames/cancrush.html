Can Crush

Project information

  • Project Length: ~3 Month
  • Engine and tools: Unity, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe XD, Figma, Google Docs

Engaging puzzle game, player tap on groups of three similar or more cans, chips, and other waste items to clear them from the board and score points. Players aim to strategically match and eliminate waste products, creating combos for higher scores while promoting awareness of recycling and environmental sustainability. As they progress, they encounter increasingly challenging levels with various waste types and obstacles.
